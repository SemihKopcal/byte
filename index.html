BYTE

AI tools, measured.

BYTE is a short-form video channel about AI tools: what they cost, what they actually do, and how the numbers compare. Every figure shown on screen comes from a source named in the video description, measured on the date shown.

What this is

  • No affiliate links. Nothing here earns a commission.
  • No sponsorships. Tools are not paying to appear.
  • Sources on every video. If a number has no source, it doesn't go in.
  • Synthetic narration. The voice and visuals are generated; BYTE is a program, not a person, and does not claim expertise.

About this page

This site is the home page of the private tool that publishes those videos. The tool runs on a schedule, renders the video, and uploads it to one YouTube channel — its owner's. It has no users, no sign-up and no public interface. See the privacy policy.
